What counts as a good CPS?

Most people click between 4 and 6 times per second on a standard test. Anything above 7 CPS is considered fast, and scores past 10 CPS usually come from techniques like jitter clicking or butterfly clicking rather than a normal single-finger click.

What is a click speed test used for?

It's most often used by gamers to check reaction speed and mouse performance for games like Minecraft PvP, first-person shooters, and rhythm games, but it also works as a quick way to test whether a mouse's clicks are being registered properly.

How is CPS calculated?

CPS stands for clicks per second. It's calculated by dividing your total number of clicks by the total time of the test, so 30 clicks in a 5 second test equals 6 CPS.

Why does my score change between 5 second and 60 second tests?

Short tests tend to produce higher CPS because it's easier to click quickly in a short burst than to sustain that speed. Longer tests are a better measure of consistent clicking speed over time.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes, the test responds to taps as well as mouse clicks, so you can run it on a phone or tablet screen too.
