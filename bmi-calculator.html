The category ranges (WHO standard)

  • Under 18.5 — underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — normal weight
  • 25 – 29.9 — overweight
  • 30 and above — obese

Worth being upfront about the limits here: BMI only looks at height and weight, so it can't tell muscle from fat. An athlete with low body fat can easily land in "overweight" territory just from muscle mass — the number is a population-level screening tool, not a verdict on any one person's health.

Metric or imperial — does it matter which I use?
