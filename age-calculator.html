How age is calculated here

Your age in years, months, and days is calculated by comparing your birth date to today's date and accounting for varying month lengths and leap years, not just a simple day count divided evenly.

Why do months and days sometimes look uneven?

Because months have different numbers of days, the months and days shown represent the actual calendar distance from your last birthday, not a fixed 30-day approximation.

Does this account for leap years?

Yes, the total day count factors in leap years automatically since it's based on real calendar dates rather than an estimate.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No, the calculation happens entirely in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
