What this site is

A small set of free browser tools for testing gaming reflexes, plus everyday calculators.

I started building this because I kept bouncing between different sites to check my click speed, reaction time, and a few everyday calculations, and most of the ones I found were cluttered with ads or looked like they hadn't been touched since 2012. Reflex Drill started as just a click speed test, and grew from there — a spacebar counter, a reaction time test, an aim trainer, and eventually some calculators I kept needing anyway (GPA, BMI, that kind of thing).

What it actually is

Ten free tools, no account, no download. Pick one, use it, see your result. That's the whole idea — I'd rather have a handful of things that work properly than a hundred things bolted together.

How it's built

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type or click gets uploaded or saved anywhere on my end — your results exist for that one session and disappear when you close the tab.

Still adding to it

This is a project I keep coming back to, not something I built once and left alone. If a tool feels off or you've got an idea for something worth adding, the Contact page reaches me directly.
