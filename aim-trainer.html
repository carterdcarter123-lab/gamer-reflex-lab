What does this measure?

This tool tracks two things: how many targets you can hit in the time given, and how accurate your clicks are overall. A high hit count with low accuracy usually means you're clicking too fast and missing often, while high accuracy with fewer hits suggests being more careful than necessary.

Why do misses count against accuracy?

Every click inside the play area counts as an attempt. If a click doesn't land on the target, it's recorded as a miss, and accuracy is calculated as hits divided by total clicks.

Do targets get faster over time?

No, target spawn timing stays consistent throughout a single run so your score reflects raw aim rather than a difficulty curve.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes, tapping the targets works the same as clicking on desktop.

How does this help with FPS aim?

Shooter games reward both quick target acquisition and precision. Practicing on random, unpredictable target positions like this trains the same hand-eye coordination used to track and hit moving or newly appearing targets in a match.

Should I prioritize speed or accuracy?

Early on, accuracy matters more — clicking wildly to boost your hit count usually tanks your accuracy percentage and doesn't reflect real improvement. Once your accuracy is consistently high, focus on increasing speed without letting it drop.
