Random number generator

Set your range and how many numbers you need, then generate.

What people actually use this for

  • Picking a raffle or giveaway winner from a range of ticket numbers
  • Settling "who goes first" in a game night without an argument
  • Generating sample data for a classroom exercise or spreadsheet test
  • Rolling a quick random number when you just don't want to decide something yourself

This uses your browser's standard randomization — good enough for all of the above, but not built for anything security-sensitive like generating passwords or cryptographic keys.

Asked for more unique numbers than the range has?

You'll get back as many as actually fit — asking for 10 unique numbers between 1 and 5 will just return those 5.
