Why test your reflexes?

Clicking speed, reaction time, and key-press speed all matter differently depending on what you play. Minecraft PvP rewards fast, sustained clicking. Shooters and rhythm games reward quick reaction to visual cues. Some games use rapid spacebar presses for jumping, charging, or mashing mechanics. Testing each one separately gives you a clearer picture of where your actual strengths are.

Which test should I try first?

If you're not sure, start with the Click Speed Test — it's the most common benchmark and the easiest to compare against typical scores.

Do these tests work on mobile?

The Click Speed Test and Reaction Time Test both work with taps on a phone or tablet. The Spacebar Counter needs a physical keyboard, so it's built for desktop.

Are my scores saved?

Not yet — each test currently shows your result for that session only. Refreshing or leaving the page resets it.
