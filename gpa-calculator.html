The grade scale this uses

Grades convert to points on the standard 4.0 scale, then each course's points get weighted by its credit hours before averaging:

  • A / A+ — 4.0, A- — 3.7
  • B+ — 3.3, B — 3.0, B- — 2.7
  • C+ — 2.3, C — 2.0, C- — 1.7
  • D+ — 1.3, D — 1.0, D- — 0.7
  • F — 0.0

A 4-credit course pulling a B counts for more than a 1-credit course pulling an A — that's the whole reason credit-weighting matters instead of just averaging the letters directly.

Semester GPA or cumulative GPA — which is this?

Whichever you want. Enter just this term's courses for a semester GPA, or everything you've ever taken for a cumulative one. The math doesn't change, only what you put in.

This isn't weighted GPA (with AP/honors bonus points) — why?

Weighted scales vary a lot by school, and going above 4.0 gets confusing fast. This sticks to the standard scale every school recognizes, which is usually what's actually needed for basic tracking.
