What's a good spacebar speed?

Most people can press the spacebar between 5 and 8 times per second in short bursts. Games that involve mashing the spacebar for charging, jumping, or building momentum reward both raw speed and being able to sustain it over a longer test.

Why do I need to click the box first?

The page needs to know you're ready to interact with the test area before it starts counting spacebar presses, otherwise pressing space while browsing the page would trigger the test by accident.

Does this work on mobile?

No, this test needs a physical spacebar key, so it's built for desktop and laptop keyboards rather than touchscreens.

How is presses per second calculated?

It works the same way as a click speed test: your total spacebar presses are divided by the total test duration, so 40 presses in 10 seconds equals 4 presses per second.

Is this the same as the spacebar counter challenge?

Yes, this is the same type of test behind the spacebar-tapping challenge that circulated on TikTok, where people compare how many times they can hit the spacebar in a set time.

Is spacebar speed different from click speed?

They use different muscles and often produce different scores. Some people press a key faster than they click a mouse button, and vice versa, so it's worth testing both separately rather than assuming they'll match.
