What's a good typing speed?

Average typing speed is around 40 words per minute. Proficient typists usually land between 60 and 80 WPM, and professional typists or competitive typing players can exceed 100 WPM, often while keeping accuracy above 97%.

How is WPM calculated?

Words per minute is calculated by dividing the number of correctly typed characters by 5 (the average word length), then dividing by the time elapsed in minutes.

Does accuracy affect my WPM score?

Yes, only correctly typed characters count toward your WPM. Mistyped characters lower both your accuracy percentage and your effective speed.

Why does the passage change each time?

A new passage is chosen for each attempt so you can't improve your score simply by memorizing the text.

What's considered a fast typing speed?

Professional typists and competitive typing players often exceed 100 WPM while keeping accuracy above 97%. For most people, breaking 70 WPM comfortably puts you well ahead of the average office worker.

How can I improve my typing speed?

Consistent short practice sessions tend to help more than occasional long ones. Focusing on typing without looking at the keyboard (touch typing) and slowing down slightly to reduce errors usually raises your effective WPM faster than just trying to type harder.
